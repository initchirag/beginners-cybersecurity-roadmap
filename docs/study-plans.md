# Study plans

| Pace | Weekly pattern | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 30 min reading + 90 min lab, 3 days | 12–18 months |
| Steady | 2 × 60 min study + 2 × 90 min lab | 8–12 months |
| Intensive | 5 focused sessions plus review | 4–8 months |

Every session: define an outcome, take notes, make one reproducible artifact,
and record what remains unclear. Reserve time for rest and accessibility needs.

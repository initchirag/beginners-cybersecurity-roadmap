# Certifications

Certifications complement hands-on evidence; check the issuer for current
exam objectives, pricing, and renewal rules.

| Stage | Options | Official information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fundamentals | ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity, CompTIA Security+ | [ISC2](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc), [CompTIA](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security) |
| Networking/cloud | Cisco CCNA, AWS/Azure/GCP associate security paths | [Cisco](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/learn/training-certifications/certifications/), [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/), [Azure](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/) |
| Operations | CompTIA CySA+, GIAC (role-specific) | [CySA+](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/cybersecurity-analyst), [GIAC](https://www.giac.org/) |
| Testing | OffSec OSCP, CREST (region-specific) | [OffSec](https://www.offsec.com/certifications/oscp-certification/), [CREST](https://www.crest-approved.org/) |
| Senior/leadership | CISSP, CISM | [ISC2 CISSP](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp), [ISACA CISM](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism) |

Do not treat any certificate as proof of authorization or practical mastery.

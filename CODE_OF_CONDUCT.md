# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, patient and technically honest.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith.
- Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Welcome beginners.
- Credit original work.
- Respect privacy.
- Keep security discussions within lawful and authorized boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential theft, malicious payload sharing, unauthorized-target instructions, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's issue/contact mechanisms.
